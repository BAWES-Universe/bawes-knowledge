# Your Brick — how it works

## Your Brick

Your brick is your own AI worker, running 24/7 on the fleet. It lives in your DM thread — that thread becomes your brick channel.

### Activate it
Reply **yes** when it asks for your consent (it will ask once, in your own words). That's the only "setup" there is.

### What it does for you
- **Research** — ask it to look things up (it reads live pages, not stale answers)
- **Images & music** — it can generate visuals and audio on request
- **Receipts & wallet** — it tracks your spend and earnings in bananas (🍌)
- **Your data, your keys** — optionally connect your own API keys (OpenRouter or similar): vaulted, spend-capped, revocable, never shared. Your key = your lane; if you list it, you earn when others use it.

### Bananas (🍌)
1🍌 = $0.01. You start with a seed balance; you earn by contributing (reviews, verified visits, quests) and spend on tool use. Your wallet is visible to you — nothing hidden.

### Privacy
Your brick never claims things it can't prove. No secrets leave your thread unless you say so. Anything it says about itself (what it can do, what happened) is verified, not imagined.
