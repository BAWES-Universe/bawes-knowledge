# Your Brick — how it works

## Your Brick

Your brick is your own AI worker, running 24/7. It lives in your DM thread — that thread becomes your brick channel.

### Activate it
Reply **yes** when it asks for your consent (once, in your own words). That's the only setup.

### What it does for you
- **Research** — live page reads, not stale answers
- **Images & music** — generate visuals and audio on request
- **Receipts & wallet** — spend and earnings tracked in bananas (🍌)
- **BYOK** — connect your own keys (OpenRouter or similar): vaulted, spend-capped, revocable, never shared. Your key = your lane; list it and earn when others use it.

### Bananas (🍌)
1🍌 = $0.01. Seed balance to start; earn by contributing (reviews, verified visits, quests); spend on tools. Wallet always visible.

### Privacy
No unverifiable claims. Nothing leaves your thread without your say-so. Everything the brick says about itself is verified.
